Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

VesyCreates (Vesna, Slovenia) uses its apps only to manage its own social media accounts (Pinterest, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube @vesycreates): reading statistics of its own posts, pins and boards, and publishing its own handmade embroidery tutorials.

What data the apps use

  • The apps access only the VesyCreates accounts. They do not access, collect or store data of any other users.
  • Statistics read from the platforms are stored privately on the owner's computer and are used only to plan content. They are never sold or shared with third parties.
  • Access tokens are stored privately on the owner's computer and are never shared.

Your choices

The owner can revoke the apps' access at any time in the settings of each platform.
