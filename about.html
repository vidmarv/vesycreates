Hi, I'm Vesna! Welcome to my creative world. :)

I'm a creative soul from Slovenia who believes there's something truly special about making things with your own hands. Give me some thread, a needle, wool, or a bundle of cords, and I'm happy! I love exploring different crafts, experimenting with new techniques, and discovering how a few simple materials can become something beautiful and full of personality.

My creative journey is all about curiosity, learning, and the joy of making. I enjoy embroidery, embroidery on paper, needle felting, macramé, and all sorts of DIY projects. I don't believe you have to be an expert or have a natural talent to create something wonderful. Sometimes, all you need is a little inspiration, some patience, and the courage to give it a try.

And here's one of my favourite parts: I love sharing what I learn with you! For me, creativity becomes even more meaningful when it brings people together. That's why I create free YouTube tutorials and share short, inspiring reels on Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok, to show you how things are made, help you learn something new, and encourage you to discover your own creative side.

My goal isn't just to show you the finished project. I want you to feel that you can make it, too! Whether you're picking up a needle for the first time or looking for your next creative adventure, there's a place for you here.

So, make yourself at home, grab a cup of your favourite drink, and let's spend some time creating. There are so many lovely things we can make together!

A little creativity, a bit of patience, and lots of handmade love. That's my kind of world.

I'm so happy you're here! 💜
